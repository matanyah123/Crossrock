## Mojang Bedrock vanilla flipbook reference

`src/main/resources/bedrock_compat/mappings/mojang_vanilla_flipbook_textures.json` is a JSON-only
copy of the vanilla animation definitions published in Mojang's Bedrock samples. It is used as an
internal compatibility mapping; it is not installed into, or presented as, a Bedrock resource pack.

Source: https://github.com/Mojang/bedrock-samples/blob/main/resource_pack/textures/flipbook_textures.json

Source commit: `736072450c26a7c67f07b1661f29d9a5ebaa14b1`
